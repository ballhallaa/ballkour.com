    Courses

    1. In Overwatch, open Play → More → Custom Games → Create.
    2. Go to Settings → Import Code (under Summary), and paste the code (e.g. 59W28).
    3. Start the match and enjoy!

      Features

      • One continuous run

        Clear every checkpoint in order, from start to finish, with one total time at the end.

      • Mistake counter

        Missing a checkpoint or dying to the map counts. Your total sits next to your time.

      • Checkpoint rules

        Some checkpoints block or utilize only Grapple, Shield Bounce, and/or Piledriver.

      • Skip, go back, freemode

        Practice any checkpoint, or roam the map with the clock paused.

      • Quality of life

        Hold to restart a checkpoint or your whole run, pause anytime and resume later, and get a bhop of grace on every checkpoint. Beta Idle runners shrink so they never block your view.

      • Lobby leaderboard

        On the Ballkour remakes, the top 10 times hang in the lobby and the finish screen shows the time to beat.

      • Hints Beta

        A recorded route shows you the way through. Turn hints on yourself, or they switch on after 10 mistakes on one checkpoint.

      • Splits Beta

        Best and average times for every checkpoint, so you can see where you lose time.

      • Pace Beta

        Your estimated finish time, updated live from your average split. It turns red when you fall behind.

      • Spectate Beta

        Watch other runners in first or third person, or fly in freecam mode.

      • Scorecards Beta

        See every runner’s checkpoint, time, and mistakes live, along with the lobby’s top 3.

      • Translations Beta

        Translated into 13 languages, so each player gets the full experience in their own language.
