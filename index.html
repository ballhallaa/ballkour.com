      Features

      • Checkpoint rules

        Some checkpoints block or utilize only Grapple, Shield Bounce, and/or Piledriver.

      • Skip, go back, freemode

        Practice any checkpoint, or roam the map with the clock paused.

      • Quality of life

        A cleaner HUD with easier menu controls. Hold to restart a checkpoint or your whole run, pause anytime and resume later, and get a bhop of grace on every checkpoint.

      • Lobby leaderboard

        The top 10 times hang in the lobby and the finish screen shows the time to beat.

      BetaTry these with a Beta code from the course list.

      • Hints

        A recorded route shows you the way through. Turn hints on yourself, or they switch on after 10 mistakes on one checkpoint.

      • Spectate

        Watch other runners in first or third person, or fly in freecam mode.

      • Splits

        Best and average times for every checkpoint, plus a pace timer to see your estimated finish time.

      • Assist Mode

        Every ability as often as you like, free usage of the ground, while your timer runs and checkpoints still clear. Assisted runs have their own leaderboards.

      • Scorecards

        See every player’s checkpoint, time, and mistakes live, along with the lobby’s top 3.

      • Customize HUD

        Turn any line of the HUD on or off from the lobby, so it only shows what you want to see.

      • More quality of life

        Idle runners shrink so they never block your view. See your current run and attempt numbers, view each run’s video and date in the lobby, and toggle an FAQ for newcomers.

      • Translations

        Translated into 13 languages, so each player gets a similar experience in their own language.

      On ballkour.com

      • My profile

        Sign in with Discord to see your rating, place, and Top %, every course you’ve run, and all your runs. Share them with a link, or show them on your Discord profile.

      • Player ratings

        Every course is worth up to 1,000 points, and your rating is your average over all of them. Your Top % is where you place among every rated player, and you automatically receive the role on our Discord.
